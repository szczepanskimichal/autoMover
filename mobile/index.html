Use From Phone

1. On the Mac run `./scripts/run_full_session.sh --recreate` once if the container is old. 2. Run `./scripts/run_mobile_control.sh`. 3. Open the printed `http://...:8080` URL on the phone. 4. In Foxglove on the phone or another device, connect to `ws://MAC_IP:8765`.

The page publishes `/cmd_vel`, `/work_mode`, `/engine_enabled`, `/tool_enabled`, `/tool_power`, `/tool_angle`, `/simulate_lidar_obstacle`, and `/emergency_stop`. It also subscribes to `/mower/status_text`, `/mower/blade_rpm`, `/mower/cut_height`, `/mower/lidar_obstacle`, and `/mower/safety_stop`.
